Is $31.50 an hour good?

$31.50 an hour is right around the US median full-time wage. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that MIT's Living Wage Calculator estimates a single adult with no children needs on average in the US.

In practice it means about $4,540 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 35% of take-home — above the 30% guideline, workable with a careful budget. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $2,490 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: elementary school teachers ($30.62/hr), plumbers ($30.27/hr), electricians ($29.98/hr), graphic designers ($29.47/hr).

How it compares

$31.50 an hour is 4.3× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 5% above the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
