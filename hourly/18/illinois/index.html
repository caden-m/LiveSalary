Illinois income tax on $18 an hour

Illinois has a flat 4.95% income tax after a $2,850 standard deduction. Flat 4.95%. Deduction is the $2,850 personal exemption (no standard deduction). Chicago minimum wage is higher.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Illinois costs about $1,712 a year on this income — $143 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $32,122.

How $18 an hour compares in Illinois

$18 an hour is 1.2× the Illinois minimum wage of $15 and below the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Illinois is about $83,211 (US: $80,610), so $18 an hour from one earner is 45% of a typical household's income here.
