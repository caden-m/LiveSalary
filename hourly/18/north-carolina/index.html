North Carolina income tax on $18 an hour

North Carolina has a flat 4.25% income tax after a $12,750 standard deduction. Flat 4.25% for 2025 (3.99% in 2026). $12,750 single standard deduction.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in North Carolina costs about $1,049 a year on this income — $87 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $32,122.

How $18 an hour compares in North Carolina

$18 an hour is 2.5× the North Carolina minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and below the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in North Carolina is about $73,958 (US: $80,610), so $18 an hour from one earner is 51% of a typical household's income here.
