Iowa income tax on $26.50 an hour

Iowa has a flat 3.80% income tax after a $15,000 standard deduction. Newly flat 3.8% in 2025. Iowa starts from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 pre-OBBBA; $15,750 if the state follows the 2025 federal increase). $40 personal credit (not modeled). Many school districts add a surtax on state tax (a few percent of the state tax).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Iowa costs about $1,525 a year on this income — $127 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $46,327.

How $26.50 an hour compares in Iowa

$26.50 an hour is 3.7× the Iowa minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and below the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Iowa is about $75,501 (US: $80,610), so $26.50 an hour from one earner is 73% of a typical household's income here.
