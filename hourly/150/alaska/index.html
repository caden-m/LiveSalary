Alaska income tax on $150 an hour

Alaska does not tax wage income. No state income tax. Minimum wage $11.91 from Jan 1, 2025, rising to $13.00 on July 1, 2025.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Alaska costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $222,053.

How $150 an hour compares in Alaska

$150 an hour is 12.6× the Alaska minimum wage of $11.91 and above the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Alaska is about $95,665 (US: $80,610), so $150 an hour from one earner is 326% of a typical household's income here.
