New Hampshire income tax on $150 an hour

New Hampshire does not tax wage income. No tax on wages. The interest and dividends tax was repealed starting with 2025, so there is no personal income tax in 2025. No state minimum wage beyond the federal $7.25.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in New Hampshire costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $222,315.

How $150 an hour compares in New Hampshire

$150 an hour is 20.7× the New Hampshire minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in New Hampshire is about $99,782 (US: $81,604), so $150 an hour from one earner is 313% of a typical household's income here.
