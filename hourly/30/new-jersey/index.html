New Jersey income tax on $30 an hour

New Jersey uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $1,000 standard deduction. Deduction is the $1,000 personal exemption (no standard deduction). Newark levies a 1% payroll tax on employers, not employees. Minimum wage $15.49 for employers with 6+ employees.

How $30 an hour compares in New Jersey

$30 an hour is 1.9× the New Jersey minimum wage of $15.49 and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in New Jersey is about $104,294 (US: $81,604), so $30 an hour from one earner is 60% of a typical household's income here.
