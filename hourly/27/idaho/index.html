Idaho income tax on $27 an hour

Idaho has a flat 5.30% income tax after a $20,561 standard deduction. Flat 5.3% for 2025 (cut from 5.695% by HB 40, 2025). Idaho taxes the first $4,811 of taxable income at 0%; that zero bracket is folded into the deduction ($15,000 federal standard deduction + $4,811). Also a $155 grocery credit (not modeled). HB 559 (Feb 2026, retroactive to 2025) adopts the federal $15,750 standard deduction; plus the $4,811 zero bracket.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Idaho costs about $1,887 a year on this income — $157 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $47,253.

How $27 an hour compares in Idaho

$27 an hour is 3.7× the Idaho minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Idaho is about $81,166 (US: $81,604), so $27 an hour from one earner is 69% of a typical household's income here.
