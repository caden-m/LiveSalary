Maryland income tax on $45 an hour

Maryland uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $6,550 standard deduction. 2025 changes (HB 352, retroactive to Jan 1, 2025): new 6.25% and 6.5% top brackets and a flat $3,350 standard deduction. Deduction = $3,350 + $3,200 personal exemption. All counties and Baltimore City add a local income tax of 2.25% to 3.3%.

How $45 an hour compares in Maryland
