Iowa income tax on $63 an hour

Iowa has a flat 3.80% income tax after a $15,000 standard deduction. Newly flat 3.8% in 2025. Iowa starts from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 pre-OBBBA; $15,750 if the state follows the 2025 federal increase). $40 personal credit (not modeled). Many school districts add a surtax on state tax (a few percent of the state tax).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Iowa costs about $4,410 a year on this income — $367 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $100,319.

How $63 an hour compares in Iowa

$63 an hour is 8.7× the Iowa minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Iowa is about $75,501 (US: $80,610), so $63 an hour from one earner is 174% of a typical household's income here.
