Montana income tax on $11 an hour

Montana uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $15,000 standard deduction. Montana starts from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 as published for 2025).

How $11 an hour compares in Montana

$11 an hour is 1.0× the Montana minimum wage of $10.55 and below the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Montana is about $75,340 (US: $80,610), so $11 an hour from one earner is 30% of a typical household's income here.
