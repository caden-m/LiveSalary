Indiana income tax on $16.50 an hour

Indiana has a flat 3.00% income tax after a $1,000 standard deduction. Flat 3.0% for 2025. Deduction is the $1,000 personal exemption. All 92 counties levy a local income tax (roughly 0.5% to 3.4%, about 1.5% to 2% typical).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Indiana costs about $1,000 a year on this income — $83 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $29,705.

How $16.50 an hour compares in Indiana

$16.50 an hour is 2.3× the Indiana minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Indiana is about $71,959 (US: $81,604), so $16.50 an hour from one earner is 48% of a typical household's income here.
