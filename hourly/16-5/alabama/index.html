Alabama income tax on $16.50 an hour

Alabama uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $4,000 standard deduction. Deduction = $1,500 personal exemption + $2,500 standard deduction (the $3,000 single standard deduction phases down to $2,500 above about $33,000 AGI). Federal income tax paid is deductible on the AL return (not modeled). A few cities (Birmingham, Bessemer, Gadsden etc.) levy occupational taxes. No state minimum wage; federal $7.25 applies.
