North Dakota income tax on $28 an hour

North Dakota uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $15,750 standard deduction. Starts from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 pre-OBBBA; $15,750 if the state follows the 2025 federal increase). The first $48,475 of taxable income is taxed at 0%. Uses the 2025 federal standard deduction of $15,750 (starts from federal taxable income; not separately confirmed for 2025).
