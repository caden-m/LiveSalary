Washington income tax on $21.50 an hour

Washington does not tax wage income. No tax on wages. A 7% tax applies to long-term capital gains over about $270,000 (9.9% over $1M from 2025). Employees pay WA Cares (0.58%) and paid-leave premiums (not income taxes).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Washington costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $37,971.

How $21.50 an hour compares in Washington

$21.50 an hour is 1.3× the Washington minimum wage of $16.66 and below the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Washington is about $99,389 (US: $80,610), so $21.50 an hour from one earner is 45% of a typical household's income here.
