Maine income tax on $65 an hour

Maine uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $20,150 standard deduction. Deduction = $15,000 standard deduction + $5,150 personal exemption; both phase out at high incomes.

How $65 an hour compares in Maine

$65 an hour is 4.4× the Maine minimum wage of $14.65 and above the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Maine is about $76,442 (US: $80,610), so $65 an hour from one earner is 177% of a typical household's income here.
