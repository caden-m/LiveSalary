California income tax on $26 an hour

California uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $5,706 standard deduction. 2025 brackets and $5,706 standard deduction. An extra 1% Mental Health Services Tax applies to taxable income over $1M (13.3% top). Also a $153 single personal exemption credit (not modeled). Employees pay ~1.2% SDI with no wage cap (not an income tax).

How $26 an hour compares in California

$26 an hour is 1.6× the California minimum wage of $16.50 and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in California is about $100,149 (US: $81,604), so $26 an hour from one earner is 54% of a typical household's income here.
