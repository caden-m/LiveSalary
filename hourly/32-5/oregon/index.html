Oregon income tax on $32.50 an hour

Oregon uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $2,835 standard deduction. Also a personal exemption credit of about $250 (not modeled). Portland-area residents with income over $125,000 pay Metro SHS (1%) and Multnomah County Preschool for All (1.5% and up) taxes.
