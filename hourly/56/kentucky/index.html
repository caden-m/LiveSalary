Kentucky income tax on $56 an hour

Kentucky has a flat 4.00% income tax after a $3,270 standard deduction. Flat 4.0% for 2025 (3.5% in 2026). Most cities and many counties levy occupational license taxes on wages (often 1% to 2.5%; Louisville about 2.2%).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Kentucky costs about $4,528 a year on this income — $377 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $90,330.

How $56 an hour compares in Kentucky

$56 an hour is 7.7× the Kentucky minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Kentucky is about $64,526 (US: $80,610), so $56 an hour from one earner is 181% of a typical household's income here.
