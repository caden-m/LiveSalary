Arizona income tax on $24 an hour

Arizona has a flat 2.50% income tax after a $15,750 standard deduction. Flat 2.5%. Arizona's standard deduction matches the federal amount ($15,000 as published for 2025; $15,750 if Arizona adopts the 2025 federal increase). Uses the 2025 federal standard deduction of $15,750 (2025 Form 140 booklet, per Nov 2025 executive order).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Arizona costs about $854 a year on this income — $71 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $42,239.

How $24 an hour compares in Arizona

$24 an hour is 1.6× the Arizona minimum wage of $14.70 and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Arizona is about $81,486 (US: $81,604), so $24 an hour from one earner is 61% of a typical household's income here.
