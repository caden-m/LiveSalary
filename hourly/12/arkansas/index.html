Arkansas income tax on $12 an hour

Arkansas uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $2,410 standard deduction. Simplified to the high-income schedule (2% then 3.9% top rate); lower-income filers use separate tables with lower tax. Plus a $29 personal tax credit (not modeled).
