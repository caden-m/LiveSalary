New Hampshire income tax on $175 an hour

New Hampshire does not tax wage income. No tax on wages. The interest and dividends tax was repealed starting with 2025, so there is no personal income tax in 2025. No state minimum wage beyond the federal $7.25.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in New Hampshire costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $254,893.
