Connecticut income tax on $500 an hour

Connecticut uses progressive brackets on taxable income. No standard deduction. The $15,000 personal exemption phases out between $30,000 and $44,000 AGI, so it is 0 for a typical earner. The 2% bracket benefit is recaptured at higher incomes and there is a small property-tax/personal credit (not modeled).
