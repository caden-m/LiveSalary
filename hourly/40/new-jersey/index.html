New Jersey income tax on $40 an hour

New Jersey uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $1,000 standard deduction. Deduction is the $1,000 personal exemption (no standard deduction). Newark levies a 1% payroll tax on employers, not employees. Minimum wage $15.49 for employers with 6+ employees.
