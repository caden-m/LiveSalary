Is $225 an hour good?

$225 an hour is far above the US median — top-percentile pay. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that MIT's Living Wage Calculator estimates a single adult with no children needs on average in the US.

In practice it means about $26,649 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 6% of take-home, under the 30% guideline, leaving room to save. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $24,599 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: family medicine physicians ($115.76/hr), airline pilots, copilots and flight engineers ($108.94/hr), dentists ($86.16/hr).

How it compares

$225 an hour is 31.0× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 653% above the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
