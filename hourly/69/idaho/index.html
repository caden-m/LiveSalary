Idaho income tax on $69 an hour

Idaho has a flat 5.30% income tax after a $19,811 standard deduction. Flat 5.3% for 2025 (cut from 5.695% by HB 40, 2025). Idaho taxes the first $4,811 of taxable income at 0%; that zero bracket is folded into the deduction ($15,000 federal standard deduction + $4,811). Also a $155 grocery credit (not modeled).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Idaho costs about $6,557 a year on this income — $546 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $108,849.

How $69 an hour compares in Idaho

$69 an hour is 9.5× the Idaho minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Idaho is about $81,166 (US: $80,610), so $69 an hour from one earner is 177% of a typical household's income here.
