Oregon income tax on $69 an hour

Oregon uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $2,835 standard deduction. Also a personal exemption credit of about $250 (not modeled). Portland-area residents with income over $125,000 pay Metro SHS (1%) and Multnomah County Preschool for All (1.5% and up) taxes. Minimum wage $14.70 standard; $15.05 from July 1, 2025 (Portland metro higher). 2025 Publication OR-17: $2,835 standard deduction, brackets to $4,400 / $11,100 / $125,000; $256 exemption credit (not modeled) ends above $100k AGI.
