Colorado income tax on $17.50 an hour

Colorado has a flat 4.40% income tax after a $15,750 standard deduction. Flat 4.4%, starting from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 pre-OBBBA; $15,750 if the state follows the 2025 federal increase). Denver, Aurora, Glendale, Greenwood Village and Sheridan levy small monthly occupational privilege taxes (not modeled). Uses the 2025 federal standard deduction of $15,750 (starts from federal taxable income).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Colorado costs about $909 a year on this income — $76 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $31,376.
