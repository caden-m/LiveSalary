Montana income tax on $25 an hour

Montana uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $15,750 standard deduction. Montana starts from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 as published for 2025). Uses the 2025 federal standard deduction of $15,750 (2025 Form 2 instructions).

How $25 an hour compares in Montana

$25 an hour is 2.4× the Montana minimum wage of $10.55 and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Montana is about $75,340 (US: $81,604), so $25 an hour from one earner is 69% of a typical household's income here.
