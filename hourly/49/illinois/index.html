Illinois income tax on $49 an hour

Illinois has a flat 4.95% income tax after a $2,850 standard deduction. Flat 4.95%. Deduction is the $2,850 personal exemption (no standard deduction). Chicago minimum wage is higher.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Illinois costs about $4,904 a year on this income — $409 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $80,252.

How $49 an hour compares in Illinois
