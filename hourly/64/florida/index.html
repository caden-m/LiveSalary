Florida income tax on $64 an hour

Florida does not tax wage income. No state income tax. Minimum wage $13.00 (since Sept 30, 2024), rising to $14.00 on Sept 30, 2025.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Florida costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $101,921.

How $64 an hour compares in Florida

$64 an hour is 4.9× the Florida minimum wage of $13 and above the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Florida is about $77,735 (US: $81,604), so $64 an hour from one earner is 171% of a typical household's income here.
