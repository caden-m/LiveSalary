How $110 an hour compares in New York

$110 an hour is 7.1× the New York minimum wage of $15.50 and above the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in New York is about $85,820 (US: $81,604), so $110 an hour from one earner is 267% of a typical household's income here.
