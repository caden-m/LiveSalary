California income tax on $110 an hour

California uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $5,706 standard deduction. 2025 brackets and $5,706 standard deduction. An extra 1% Mental Health Services Tax applies to taxable income over $1M (13.3% top). Also a $153 single personal exemption credit (not modeled). Employees pay ~1.2% SDI with no wage cap (not an income tax).

How $110 an hour compares in California
