Pennsylvania income tax on $33.50 an hour

Pennsylvania has a flat 3.07% income tax. Flat 3.07% with no standard deduction or personal exemption. Nearly all municipalities and school districts levy an earned income tax (often about 1%; Philadelphia's wage tax is about 3.75% for residents).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Pennsylvania costs about $2,139 a year on this income — $178 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $57,571.

How $33.50 an hour compares in Pennsylvania

$33.50 an hour is 4.6× the Pennsylvania minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Pennsylvania is about $77,545 (US: $81,604), so $33.50 an hour from one earner is 90% of a typical household's income here.
