Missouri income tax on $36 an hour

Missouri uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $15,000 standard deduction. Top rate 4.7% for 2025. Missouri's standard deduction equals the federal amount ($15,000 as published; $15,750 after the 2025 federal increase). Kansas City and St. Louis levy a 1% earnings tax on residents and workers. Minimum wage $13.75 from Jan 1, 2025 (Proposition A).
