Louisiana income tax on $76 an hour

Louisiana has a flat 3.00% income tax after a $12,500 standard deduction. Newly flat 3.0% in 2025 with a $12,500 standard deduction (replacing the old personal exemption).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Louisiana costs about $4,367 a year on this income — $364 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $118,981.
