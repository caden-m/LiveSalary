Georgia income tax on $76 an hour

Georgia has a flat 5.19% income tax after a $12,000 standard deduction. Flat 5.19% for 2025 (cut from 5.39% retroactively by HB 111, 2025). $12,000 single standard deduction. State minimum wage is $5.15 but the federal $7.25 applies to nearly all workers.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Georgia costs about $7,582 a year on this income — $632 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $118,981.

How $76 an hour compares in Georgia

$76 an hour is 10.5× the Georgia minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Georgia is about $79,991 (US: $81,604), so $76 an hour from one earner is 198% of a typical household's income here.
