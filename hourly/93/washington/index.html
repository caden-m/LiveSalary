Washington income tax on $93 an hour

Washington does not tax wage income. No tax on wages. A 7% tax applies to long-term capital gains over about $270,000 (9.9% over $1M from 2025). Employees pay WA Cares (0.58%) and paid-leave premiums (not income taxes).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Washington costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $144,224.

How $93 an hour compares in Washington

$93 an hour is 5.6× the Washington minimum wage of $16.66 and above the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Washington is about $99,389 (US: $81,604), so $93 an hour from one earner is 195% of a typical household's income here.
