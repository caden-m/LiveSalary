How $29 an hour compares in Hawaii

$29 an hour is 2.1× the Hawaii minimum wage of $14 and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Hawaii is about $100,745 (US: $81,604), so $29 an hour from one earner is 60% of a typical household's income here.
