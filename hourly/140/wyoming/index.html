Wyoming income tax on $140 an hour

Wyoming does not tax wage income. No state income tax. State minimum wage is $5.15 but the federal $7.25 applies to nearly all workers.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Wyoming costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $209,284.
