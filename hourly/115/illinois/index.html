Illinois income tax on $115 an hour

Illinois has a flat 4.95% income tax after a $2,850 standard deduction. Flat 4.95%. Deduction is the $2,850 personal exemption (no standard deduction). Chicago minimum wage is higher.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Illinois costs about $11,699 a year on this income — $975 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $175,654.
