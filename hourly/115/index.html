Is $115 an hour good?

$115 an hour is far above the US median — top-percentile pay. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that MIT's Living Wage Calculator estimates a single adult with no children needs on average in the US.

In practice it means about $14,638 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 11% of take-home, under the 30% guideline, leaving room to save. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $12,588 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: family medicine physicians ($115.76/hr), airline pilots, copilots and flight engineers ($108.94/hr).

How it compares

$115 an hour is 15.9× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 285% above the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
