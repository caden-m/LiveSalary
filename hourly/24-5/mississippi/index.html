Mississippi income tax on $24.50 an hour

Mississippi has a flat 4.40% income tax after a $18,300 standard deduction. Flat 4.4% for 2025 on income over $10,000. Deduction = $2,300 standard deduction + $6,000 personal exemption + the $10,000 zero bracket folded in. No state minimum wage; federal $7.25 applies.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Mississippi costs about $1,437 a year on this income — $120 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $42,985.

How $24.50 an hour compares in Mississippi

$24.50 an hour is 3.4× the Mississippi minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and below the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Mississippi is about $59,127 (US: $80,610), so $24.50 an hour from one earner is 86% of a typical household's income here.
