Louisiana income tax on $8.50 an hour

Louisiana has a flat 3.00% income tax after a $12,500 standard deduction. Newly flat 3.0% in 2025 with a $12,500 standard deduction (replacing the old personal exemption).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Louisiana costs about $155 a year on this income — $13 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $16,059.

How $8.50 an hour compares in Louisiana

$8.50 an hour is 1.2× the Louisiana minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and below the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Louisiana is about $60,986 (US: $80,610), so $8.50 an hour from one earner is 29% of a typical household's income here.
