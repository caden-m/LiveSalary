Mississippi income tax on $74 an hour

Mississippi has a flat 4.40% income tax after a $18,300 standard deduction. Flat 4.4% for 2025 on income over $10,000. Deduction = $2,300 standard deduction + $6,000 personal exemption + the $10,000 zero bracket folded in. No state minimum wage; federal $7.25 applies.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Mississippi costs about $5,967 a year on this income — $497 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $116,137.

How $74 an hour compares in Mississippi

$74 an hour is 10.2× the Mississippi minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Mississippi is about $59,127 (US: $81,604), so $74 an hour from one earner is 260% of a typical household's income here.
