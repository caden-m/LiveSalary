Montana income tax on $22 an hour

Montana uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $15,750 standard deduction. Montana starts from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 as published for 2025). Uses the 2025 federal standard deduction of $15,750 (2025 Form 2 instructions).
