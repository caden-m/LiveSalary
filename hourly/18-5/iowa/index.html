Iowa income tax on $18.50 an hour

Iowa has a flat 3.80% income tax after a $15,750 standard deduction. Newly flat 3.8% in 2025. Iowa starts from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 pre-OBBBA; $15,750 if the state follows the 2025 federal increase). $40 personal credit (not modeled). Many school districts add a surtax on state tax (a few percent of the state tax). Uses the 2025 federal standard deduction of $15,750 (starts from federal taxable income).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Iowa costs about $864 a year on this income — $72 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $33,047.

How $18.50 an hour compares in Iowa

$18.50 an hour is 2.6× the Iowa minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Iowa is about $75,501 (US: $81,604), so $18.50 an hour from one earner is 51% of a typical household's income here.
