Massachusetts income tax on $160 an hour

Massachusetts uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $4,400 standard deduction. 5% flat rate plus a 4% surtax on income over $1,083,150 (2025). Deduction is the $4,400 personal exemption (no standard deduction).
