West Virginia income tax on $13.50 an hour

West Virginia uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $2,000 standard deduction. 2025 rates after a 4% trigger cut plus an extra 2% cut (top 4.82%). Deduction is the $2,000 personal exemption (no standard deduction). Some cities (e.g. Charleston, Huntington) levy flat weekly user fees (not modeled).
