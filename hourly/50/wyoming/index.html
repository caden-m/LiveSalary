Wyoming income tax on $50 an hour

Wyoming does not tax wage income. No state income tax. State minimum wage is $5.15 but the federal $7.25 applies to nearly all workers.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Wyoming costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $81,715.

How $50 an hour compares in Wyoming

$50 an hour is 6.9× the Wyoming minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Wyoming is about $75,532 (US: $81,604), so $50 an hour from one earner is 138% of a typical household's income here.
