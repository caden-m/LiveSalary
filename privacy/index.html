Privacy

Live Salary is built so that it has almost nothing to know about you. This page spells out exactly what happens to data when you use the site.

Your salary and settings stay on your device

The rate you enter, your currency, mode and theme are saved in your browser's local storage so they're there when you come back. They never leave your device: there is no account, no server-side database and no way for us to read them. Clearing your browser data, or using "Clear saved data" in Settings, removes them. Embedded counters on other websites store nothing.

No cookies

The site sets no cookies and shows no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Visitor counting

We count page views with GoatCounter, a privacy-focused service that records the page visited, the referring site, browser type, screen size and country. It does not use cookies or store IP addresses, cannot identify individuals and cannot follow you to other sites. Counts are used only to understand which pages are useful. If you use an ad blocker it will probably block this, and that's fine.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees the IP address of visitors in its server logs for operational and security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising

There are currently no ads. If ads are added to the reference pages in future, this page will be updated first with the provider and any choices available to you; the live counter page will remain ad-free.

Links to other sites

Sources such as Forbes, the BLS and the Tax Foundation are linked for transparency. Their privacy practices are their own.
