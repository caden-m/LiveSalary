A family doctor earns about $115.76 an hour

The median annual wage for family medicine physicians in the US is about $240,790 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2024 (approximate medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $115.76 an hour, $20,066 a month, or $926 a day.

Is $240,790 a year good?

$240,790 a year is far above the US median — top-percentile pay. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that MIT's Living Wage Calculator estimates a single adult with no children needs on average in the US.

In practice it means about $14,725 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 11% of take-home, under the 30% guideline, leaving room to save. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $12,675 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: airline pilots, copilots and flight engineers ($108.94/hr).

How it compares

$115.76 an hour is 16.0× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 287% above the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
