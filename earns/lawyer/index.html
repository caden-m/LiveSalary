A lawyer earns about $76.76 an hour

The median annual wage for lawyers in the US is about $159,670 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2025 (national medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $76.76 an hour, $13,306 a month, or $614 a day.

Is $159,670 a year good?

$159,670 a year is well above the US median, in the range of senior professionals and many managers. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that a single adult with no children typically needs to cover basic costs in the US, going by MIT's Living Wage Calculator state estimates (which run from the low $20s to over $30 in expensive states).

In practice it means about $10,006 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 16% of take-home, under the 30% guideline, leaving room to save. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $7,956 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: marketing managers ($80.19/hr), air traffic controllers ($71.19/hr), dentists ($84.67/hr), pharmacists ($67.75/hr).

How it compares

$76.76 an hour is 10.6× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 145% above the median full-time US wage of about $31.27 an hour ($65,052 a year).
