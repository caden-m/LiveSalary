A software developer earns about $63.98 an hour

The median annual wage for software developers in the US is about $133,080 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2024 (national medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $63.98 an hour, $11,090 a month, or $512 a day.

Is $133,080 a year good?

$133,080 a year is well above the US median, in the range of senior professionals and many managers. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that a single adult with no children typically needs to cover basic costs in the US, going by MIT's Living Wage Calculator state estimates (which run from the low $20s to over $30 in expensive states).

In practice it means about $8,491 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 19% of take-home, under the 30% guideline, leaving room to save. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $6,441 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: pharmacists ($66.10/hr), lawyers ($72.67/hr).

How it compares

$63.98 an hour is 8.8× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 105% above the median full-time US wage of about $31.27 an hour ($65,052 a year).
