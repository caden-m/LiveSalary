A software developer earns about $63.98 an hour

The median annual wage for software developers in the US is about $133,080 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2024 (approximate medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $63.98 an hour, $11,090 a month, or $512 a day.

How it compares

$63.98 an hour is 8.8× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 114% above the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
