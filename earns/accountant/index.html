A accountant earns about $39.27 an hour

The median annual wage for accountants in the US is about $81,680 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2024 (national medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $39.27 an hour, $6,807 a month, or $314 a day.

Is $81,680 a year good?

$81,680 a year is comfortably above the US median — typical of skilled trades and experienced professionals. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that a single adult with no children typically needs to cover basic costs in the US, going by MIT's Living Wage Calculator state estimates (which run from the low $20s to over $30 in expensive states).

In practice it means about $5,501 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 29% of take-home, under the 30% guideline, leaving room to save. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $3,451 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: police officers ($36.68/hr), registered nurses ($45.00/hr).

How it compares

$39.27 an hour is 5.4× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 26% above the median full-time US wage of about $31.27 an hour ($65,052 a year).
