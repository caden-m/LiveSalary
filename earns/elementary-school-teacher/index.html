A elementary school teacher earns about $30.62 an hour

The median annual wage for elementary school teachers in the US is about $63,680 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2024 (approximate medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $30.62 an hour, $5,307 a month, or $245 a day.

Is $63,680 a year good?

$63,680 a year is right around the US median full-time wage. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that MIT's Living Wage Calculator estimates a single adult with no children needs on average in the US.

In practice it means about $4,432 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 36% of take-home — above the 30% guideline, workable with a careful budget. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $2,382 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: plumbers ($30.27/hr), electricians ($29.98/hr), graphic designers ($29.47/hr), chefs and head cooks ($29.32/hr).

How it compares

$30.62 an hour is 4.2× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 2% above the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
