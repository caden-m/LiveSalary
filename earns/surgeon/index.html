A surgeon earns about $177.66 an hour

The median annual wage for surgeons in the US is about $369,540 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2025 (national medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $177.66 an hour, $30,795 a month, or $1,421 a day.

Is $369,540 a year good?

$369,540 a year is far above the US median — top-percentile pay. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that a single adult with no children typically needs to cover basic costs in the US, going by MIT's Living Wage Calculator state estimates (which run from the low $20s to over $30 in expensive states).

In practice it means about $21,530 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 7% of take-home, under the 30% guideline, leaving room to save. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $19,480 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: anesthesiologists ($188.22/hr).

How it compares

$177.66 an hour is 24.5× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 468% above the median full-time US wage of about $31.27 an hour ($65,052 a year).
