A fast food worker earns about $14.20 an hour

The median annual wage for fast food and counter workers in the US is about $29,540 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2024 (approximate medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $14.20 an hour, $2,462 a month, or $114 a day.

How it compares

$14.20 an hour is 2.0× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 53% below the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
