A chef earns about $30.03 an hour

The median annual wage for chefs and head cooks in the US is about $62,470 (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, OEWS May 2025 (national medians)). Over a standard 40-hour week that works out to $30.03 an hour, $5,206 a month, or $240 a day.

Is $62,470 a year good?

$62,470 a year is right around the US median full-time wage. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that a single adult with no children typically needs to cover basic costs in the US, going by MIT's Living Wage Calculator state estimates (which run from the low $20s to over $30 in expensive states).

In practice it means about $4,360 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 37% of take-home — above the 30% guideline, workable with a careful budget. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $2,310 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: insurance sales agents ($29.94/hr), news analysts, reporters and journalists ($29.90/hr), paralegals and legal assistants ($30.24/hr), graphic designers ($30.27/hr).

How it compares

$30.03 an hour is 4.1× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 4% below the median full-time US wage of about $31.27 an hour ($65,052 a year).
