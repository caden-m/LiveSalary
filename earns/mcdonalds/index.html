McDonald's makes about $272 in profit every second

McDonald's reported $8,563,000,000 of net income for FY2025 (ended Dec 31, 2025). Spread across every second of the year, that's $16,292 a minute, $977,511 an hour and $23,460,274 a day. On revenue of $26,885,000,000 ($853 a second), that's a 32% net margin.
