Alaska income tax on $600,000 a year

Alaska does not tax wage income. No state income tax. Minimum wage $11.91 from Jan 1, 2025, rising to $13.00 on July 1, 2025.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Alaska costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $402,747.
