South Carolina income tax on $300,000 a year

South Carolina uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $15,000 standard deduction. Top rate 6.0% for 2025 (down from 6.2%). Starts from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 pre-OBBBA; $15,750 if the state follows the 2025 federal increase). No state minimum wage; federal $7.25 applies.
