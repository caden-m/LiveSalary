Wisconsin income tax on $48,000 a year

Wisconsin uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $9,348 standard deduction. 2025 Act 15 widened the 4.4% bracket to $50,480. Wisconsin's sliding-scale standard deduction ($13,560 maximum) shrinks as income rises. $9,348 is the deduction (about $8,648) plus the $700 exemption for a single filer earning about $60,000. Lower earners get more and those above about $132,000 get none.
