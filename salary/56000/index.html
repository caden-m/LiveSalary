Is $56,000 a year good?

$56,000 a year is below the US median, in the range of many service, retail and early-career jobs. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that a single adult with no children typically needs to cover basic costs in the US, going by MIT's Living Wage Calculator state estimates (which run from the low $20s to over $30 in expensive states).

In practice it means about $3,927 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 41% of take-home — above the 30% guideline, workable with a careful budget. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $1,877 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: welders, cutters, solderers and brazers ($25.84/hr), heavy and tractor-trailer truck drivers ($28.19/hr), transit and intercity bus drivers ($28.39/hr), real estate sales agents ($25.40/hr).

How it compares

$26.92 an hour is 3.7× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 14% below the median full-time US wage of about $31.27 an hour ($65,052 a year).
