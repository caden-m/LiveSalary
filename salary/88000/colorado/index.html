Colorado income tax on $88,000 a year

Colorado has a flat 4.40% income tax after a $15,000 standard deduction. Flat 4.4%, starting from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 pre-OBBBA; $15,750 if the state follows the 2025 federal increase). Denver, Aurora, Glendale, Greenwood Village and Sheridan levy small monthly occupational privilege taxes (not modeled).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Colorado costs about $3,212 a year on this income — $268 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $70,294.
