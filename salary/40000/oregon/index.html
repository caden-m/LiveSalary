Oregon income tax on $40,000 a year

Oregon uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $2,800 standard deduction. Also a personal exemption credit of about $250 (not modeled). Portland-area residents with income over $125,000 pay Metro SHS (1%) and Multnomah County Preschool for All (1.5% and up) taxes. Minimum wage $14.70 standard; $15.05 from July 1, 2025 (Portland metro higher).
