Illinois income tax on $500,000 a year

Illinois has a flat 4.95% income tax after a $2,850 standard deduction. Flat 4.95%. Deduction is the $2,850 personal exemption (no standard deduction). Chicago minimum wage is higher.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Illinois costs about $24,609 a year on this income — $2,051 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $340,097.
