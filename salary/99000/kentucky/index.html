Kentucky income tax on $99,000 a year

Kentucky has a flat 4.00% income tax after a $3,270 standard deduction. Flat 4.0% for 2025 (3.5% in 2026). Most cities and many counties levy occupational license taxes on wages (often 1% to 2.5%; Louisville about 2.2%).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Kentucky costs about $3,829 a year on this income — $319 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $78,032.

How $99,000 a year compares in Kentucky

$99,000 a year is 6.6× the Kentucky minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and above the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Kentucky is about $64,526 (US: $80,610), so $99,000 a year from one earner is 153% of a typical household's income here.
