Is $60,000 a year good?

$60,000 a year is right around the US median full-time wage. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that MIT's Living Wage Calculator estimates a single adult with no children needs on average in the US.

In practice it means about $4,187 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 38% of take-home — above the 30% guideline, workable with a careful budget. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $2,137 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: firefighters ($28.77/hr), chefs and head cooks ($29.32/hr), graphic designers ($29.47/hr), electricians ($29.98/hr).

How it compares

$28.85 an hour is 4.0× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 4% below the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
