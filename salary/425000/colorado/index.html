Colorado income tax on $425,000 a year

Colorado has a flat 4.40% income tax after a $15,750 standard deduction. Flat 4.4%, starting from federal taxable income, so the deduction is the federal standard deduction ($15,000 pre-OBBBA; $15,750 if the state follows the 2025 federal increase). Denver, Aurora, Glendale, Greenwood Village and Sheridan levy small monthly occupational privilege taxes (not modeled).
