Washington income tax on $63,000 a year

Washington does not tax wage income. No tax on wages. A 7% tax applies to long-term capital gains over about $270,000 (9.9% over $1M from 2025). Employees pay WA Cares (0.58%) and paid-leave premiums (not income taxes).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Washington costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $52,659.

How $63,000 a year compares in Washington

$63,000 a year is 1.8× the Washington minimum wage of $16.66 and right at the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Washington is about $99,389 (US: $80,610), so $63,000 a year from one earner is 63% of a typical household's income here.
