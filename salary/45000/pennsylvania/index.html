Pennsylvania income tax on $45,000 a year

Pennsylvania has a flat 3.07% income tax. Flat 3.07% with no standard deduction or personal exemption. Nearly all municipalities and school districts levy an earned income tax (often about 1%; Philadelphia's wage tax is about 3.75% for residents).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Pennsylvania costs about $1,382 a year on this income — $115 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $38,286.

How $45,000 a year compares in Pennsylvania

$45,000 a year is 3.0× the Pennsylvania minimum wage of $7.25 (the federal rate) and below the national median full-time wage of $31.27 an hour. The median household income in Pennsylvania is about $77,545 (US: $81,604), so $45,000 a year from one earner is 58% of a typical household's income here.
