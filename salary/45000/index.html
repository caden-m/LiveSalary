Is $45,000 a year good?

$45,000 a year is below the US median, in the range of many service, retail and early-career jobs. It's below the roughly $25 an hour that MIT's Living Wage Calculator estimates a single adult with no children needs on average in the US.

In practice it means about $3,183 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 50% of take-home — far above the 30% most budgets aim for, so roommates, a cheaper area or a second income are usually part of the picture. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $1,133 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: construction laborers ($22.14/hr).

How it compares

$21.63 an hour is 3.0× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 28% below the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
