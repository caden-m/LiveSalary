Tennessee income tax on $750,000 a year

Tennessee does not tax wage income. No state income tax (Hall tax on investment income fully repealed in 2021). No state minimum wage; federal $7.25 applies.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Tennessee costs nothing extra in state income tax. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $494,564.
