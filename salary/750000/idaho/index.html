Idaho income tax on $750,000 a year

Idaho has a flat 5.30% income tax after a $19,811 standard deduction. Flat 5.3% for 2025 (cut from 5.695% by HB 40, 2025). Idaho taxes the first $4,811 of taxable income at 0%; that zero bracket is folded into the deduction ($15,000 federal standard deduction + $4,811). Also a $155 grocery credit (not modeled).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Idaho costs about $38,700 a year on this income — $3,225 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $494,287.
