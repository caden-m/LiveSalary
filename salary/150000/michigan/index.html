Michigan income tax on $150,000 a year

Michigan has a flat 4.25% income tax after a $5,800 standard deduction. Flat 4.25%. Deduction is the $5,800 personal exemption. 24 cities levy income tax (Detroit 2.4% for residents; most others 1%). Minimum wage $10.56 on Jan 1, 2025, rising to $12.48 on Feb 21, 2025.

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Michigan costs about $6,128 a year on this income — $511 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $113,278.

How $150,000 a year compares in Michigan

$150,000 a year is 6.8× the Michigan minimum wage of $10.56 and above the national median full-time wage of $29.90 an hour. The median household income in Michigan is about $72,389 (US: $80,610), so $150,000 a year from one earner is 207% of a typical household's income here.
