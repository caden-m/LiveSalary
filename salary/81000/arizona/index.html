Arizona income tax on $81,000 a year

Arizona has a flat 2.50% income tax after a $15,000 standard deduction. Flat 2.5%. Arizona's standard deduction matches the federal amount ($15,000 as published for 2025; $15,750 if Arizona adopts the 2025 federal increase).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Arizona costs about $1,650 a year on this income — $138 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $65,370.
