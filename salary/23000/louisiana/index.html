Louisiana income tax on $23,000 a year

Louisiana has a flat 3.00% income tax after a $12,500 standard deduction. Newly flat 3.0% in 2025 with a $12,500 standard deduction (replacing the old personal exemption).

Compared with a state with no income tax, living in Louisiana costs about $315 a year on this income — $26 a month. Nationally (federal taxes only), take-home would be $20,440.
