Ohio income tax on $450,000 a year

Ohio uses progressive brackets on taxable income after a $2,150 standard deduction. Ohio's 2025 schedule is 0% up to $26,050, then $342 + 2.75% over $26,050, then 3.125% over $100,000 (2.75% flat from 2026). The 0% bracket is shown as $13,614 so the fixed $342 is reproduced for incomes above $26,050. Deduction is the $2,150 personal exemption (for $40k-$80k income). Most cities and many school districts levy income tax (often 1.5% to 2.5%).
