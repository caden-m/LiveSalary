Is $8,500 a month good?

$8,500 a month is well above the US median, in the range of senior professionals and many managers. It's above the roughly $25 an hour that MIT's Living Wage Calculator estimates a single adult with no children needs on average in the US.

In practice it means about $6,679 a month after federal taxes. At that level a typical $1,600 rent would take about 24% of take-home, under the 30% guideline, leaving room to save. After rent and about $450 of groceries, roughly $4,629 a month is left for everything else: transport, insurance, phone, debt, savings.

Jobs whose median pay is close to this: mechanical engineers ($49.19/hr), registered nurses ($45.00/hr).

How it compares

$49.04 an hour is 6.8× the federal minimum wage of $7.25 and 64% above the median full-time US wage of about $29.90 an hour ($62,192 a year).
