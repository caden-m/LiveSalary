Copy this HTML

<iframe src="https://mylivesalary.com/embed/widget/?hourly=25" width="100%" height="220" style="border:0;border-radius:16px" title="Live Salary counter" loading="lazy"></iframe>

The widget is a plain iframe: it can't read anything on your page, sets no cookies and loads about 20 KB. A small "Live Salary" link in the corner is the only thing we ask for.

Tips

  • Use an annual salary instead by dividing by 2,080 (40 hours × 52 weeks) — or just pass ?annual=60000.
  • For a 24/7 earner like an athlete or a company, add &mode=clock to spread the yearly figure across every second of the year.
  • Add &theme=light for light backgrounds.
